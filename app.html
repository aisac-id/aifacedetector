        How AIFaceDetector works

        Scan a meeting (Meet / Zoom / Teams)

        1. Join your meeting in a normal browser tab.
        2. Here, set Source → Screen / meeting tab and press Share tab / screen…
        3. In Chrome's picker choose the meeting tab (or a window / whole screen).
        4. Every participant tile gets its own box: REAL, SUSPECT or SYNTHETIC. Click one to see the five signals behind the verdict.

        Scan anything you're browsing

        The same screen capture works on any tab — videos, profiles, streams. Or point the webcam source at yourself to see a live human score high.

        The five signals

        • Micro-motion — real faces constantly micro-move; frozen or rigidly-looped faces don't.
        • Blink rhythm — eye-aspect-ratio dips; avatars and stills rarely blink naturally.
        • Skin micro-texture — generative models over-smooth pores and fine grain.
        • Color richness — cel-shaded animation uses far fewer distinct colors.
        • Sensor noise — real cameras carry frame-to-frame grain; rendered or static faces don't.

        Honest limits

        This is an on-device heuristic triage tool, not a forensic deepfake classifier. Heavy video compression, beauty filters, bad lighting or a very still participant can lower a real person's score; sophisticated real-time deepfakes can pass. Treat SUSPECT as "look closer", never as proof. Everything runs locally — no frame ever leaves your machine.
